Privacy policy

Short version: we take only what we need to answer your enquiry, we do not sell it to anyone, and we delete it if you ask us to.

Who handles your details

Pride Earthworx Pty Ltd, Canberra, ACT 2615. For anything about your information, email pride.earthworx@gmail.com or call 0455 660 036.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form:

  • your name
  • your phone number
  • your email address, if you choose to leave one, because that field is optional
  • whatever you write in the job description field
  • your answers to the form questions: the work you need, the type of job, the location, what you need from us, and your timing

Nothing is collected until you press send. If you ring us or email us directly, we have only what you tell us at the time.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, look at the site if we need to, and put a price together. Nothing else. We do not send newsletters, we do not build marketing lists, and we do not use your details for advertising.

Where it is kept

Enquiries arrive at the company email address and are recorded in our own spreadsheet, both with Google (Gmail and Google Sheets). Only people in the business who work on quotes can see them.

We do not sell your details, we do not rent them out, and we do not pass them to anyone else for their own use.

How long we keep it

As long as the job is live, and afterwards while it is useful for warranty or follow up work. If an enquiry does not turn into a job, we clear it out when it is no longer of any use.

Getting it deleted

Email pride.earthworx@gmail.com and ask, and we will remove what we hold on you. You can also ask to see what we have or to have something corrected. No reason required.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies and does not run an analytics script. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Changes to this page

If how we handle enquiries changes, this page changes with it. The date below tells you when it was last touched.
