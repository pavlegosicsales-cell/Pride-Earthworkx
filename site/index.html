Common questions

Answers before you ask.

Final trim is the last cut before the surface gets handed over: the road box, the subgrade, the batter, the pad. It is the layer everyone else measures. Bulk crews move volume fast, which is a different skill to holding a design surface to a few millimetres over a long run. That is the part we do.

Both, depending on the job. We can bring plant and operator, or put an operator on your machine if you already have the gear on site. Tell us what is available and we will work to it.

Trimble Earthworks. Send us the design model and we will load it, check it against your control, and cut to it. It means grade is confirmed while the machine is working instead of waiting on a survey crew to come back and tell you it is out.

We are based in Canberra and work through the ACT and surrounding New South Wales. We also travel: we have run work in south east Queensland, the Central West and the Pilbara. If the job is worth the trip, ask us.

Most of our work is subcontract for civil contractors and developers. We do take on site cuts, pads and driveways for builders and owners where the job suits the machine. Send through what you need and we will tell you straight whether we are the right crew for it.
